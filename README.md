TradeVault

Description:
A DeFi-native escrow platform that secures cross-border trade between businesses and overseas suppliers.
Funds are locked in smart contracts and released automatically when agreed trade and delivery conditions are met.

Other strong names

- TrustPort — cross-border trade with programmable escrow.
- FlowTrade — DeFi infrastructure for safer international commerce.
- EscrowFi — decentralized escrow for global business transactions.
- TradeLock — lock funds, ship goods, release payment with verified milestones.
- BorderFi — bringing DeFi-powered settlement to global trade.
- VaultTrade — secure international commerce with programmable payments.
- ShipSure — smart-contract escrow for importers and exporters.

If this is for a hackathon, I’d pick TradeVault. It immediately communicates trade + security, while leaving room for you to build features like stablecoin payments, milestone-based escrow, dispute resolution, shipment verification, and FX conversion.
